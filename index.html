About DVIUS

DVIUS is an independent creative business based in the Netherlands, making alternative jewelry and accessories for people who want pieces that feel personal, expressive, and unapologetically different.

The work draws from gothic, fantasy, rave, queer, and alternative aesthetics. Pieces may combine handmade components, altered findings, and 3D-printed elements to create designs that are difficult to find through conventional jewelry shops.

Made to order

Selected designs can be made again on request, subject to material and component availability.

  • Necklaces and chokers
  • Earrings and ear accessories
  • Bracelets and anklets
  • Chains and body accessories
  • 3D-printed jewelry
  • Modified and embellished accessories
  • Wearable fantasy pieces
  • Small-batch custom designs
Availability varies.

Some components are limited, discontinued, vintage, or sourced in small quantities. A recreated piece may have minor differences while preserving the original design.

Custom sizing

Bodies are not standard sizes, and jewelry should not assume that they are.

Adjusted lengths

Necklace, choker, bracelet, anklet, and chain lengths may be adjusted where the design permits.

Extended sizing

Longer chains, larger closures, and alternative fits can be discussed before ordering.

Comfort and accessibility

Different fasteners, lighter components, or other practical adjustments may be possible.

Ask before ordering

Send measurements and the desired fit so feasibility and any price difference can be confirmed.

Gender-neutral and gender-affirming

DVIUS designs are not divided into “men’s” and “women’s” jewelry. Pieces are made for whoever connects with them.

Custom work can support gender expression, affirmation, and personal milestones. That may mean changing proportions, scale, styling, symbolism, colors, or fit so that a piece feels right for the person wearing it.

No justification required.

Customers do not need to explain their identity or body. Describe the look, fit, and feeling wanted, and DVIUS will determine what can be made.

Custom orders and questions

Include the type of item, preferred style, measurements, timing, and approximate budget. Reference images are useful when discussing a custom piece.
